1. Why documents are the most avoidable loss in CAP

Every year, eligible students lose reservation benefits because a document was missing, expired or simply the wrong one. Unlike a low score, this is fully avoidable: it is a preparation problem you can solve weeks before verification opens.

The core rule

A category claim is only as strong as its weakest document. Reservation is granted on verified paperwork, not on your word. If in doubt, apply early: certificate offices slow down sharply during the admission season.

2. Documents every candidate needs

  • MHT-CET scorecard for the current year
  • Class X mark sheet and passing certificate
  • Class XII mark sheet and passing certificate
  • School or college leaving certificate (LC/TC)
  • Birth certificate or other accepted proof of age
  • Aadhaar card: Aadhaar authentication was mandatory for CET 2026 registration
  • APAAR ID: in December 2025 the State CET Cell issued a notice on Aadhaar authentication, APAAR ID generation and UDID ahead of CET 2026 registration, so create your APAAR ID early
  • Domicile certificate, or the proof of Maharashtra residence that the brochure accepts
  • Recent passport-size photograph in the specified format
  • Gap certificate, only if there is a gap after Class XII

SEBC: check the current position

SEBC reservation has been subject to litigation in the High Court in recent cycles, and the State CET Cell's notices have said the SEBC policy remains subject to the Court's decision. Confirm the current position in this year's notices before relying on it.

4. Caste Certificate vs Caste Validity Certificate

DocumentWhat it establishesTiming
Caste CertificateWhich caste or tribe you belong to.Usually a one-time document.
Caste / Tribe Validity CertificateIssued by the Scrutiny Committee after separate verification; confirms the Caste Certificate is valid for reservation.Can take weeks to months; the document most likely to cause a last-minute scramble.
The mistake

Submitting only the Caste Certificate and assuming it is enough. Most reserved-category claims need both. Apply for the validity certificate as early as you can.

What happened in 2026: extra time after admission

A Social Justice and Special Assistance Department circular dated 4 September 2026 gave SC, VJ/DT, NT and SBC candidates who had secured admission three more months to submit their Caste Validity Certificate. Relief like this is decided year by year, so do not plan around it; if your certificate is still in process, ask the Facilitation Centre what interim proof this year's rules accept.

5. The Non-Creamy-Layer (NCL) trap

For OBC, SEBC, VJ and NT candidates, the NCL certificate confirms your family is below the "creamy layer" limit. The detail that catches students out is its validity.

The validity trap

An NCL certificate is ordinarily valid only up to 31 March of the admission year, not for a full year from its issue date. A certificate issued last year for something else has very likely expired for this admission. Get or renew it specifically for this admission year.

6. EWS, PwD, Defence and TFWS

EWS (Economically Weaker Section)

Needs an EWS Eligibility Certificate for the current financial year, based on the income and asset limits in the applicable government notification. It is separate from, and not interchangeable with, the TFWS income certificate.

PwD (Persons with Disability)

Needs a disability certificate from a competent medical authority showing the prescribed percentage. The 2025 notice ahead of CET 2026 registration also covered UDID (Unique Disability ID), so get it if you do not have one. PwD codes combine with your underlying category, so you need both sets of documents.

Defence

Needs proof of the parent's or candidate's defence service, such as a service certificate or discharge book, from the appropriate authority. Defence codes also combine with the underlying category.

TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme)

Needs a family income certificate for the current financial year below the limit, commonly ₹8 lakh a year (confirm in this year's notification). TFWS is chosen within CAP rather than at registration, and even with tuition waived some development or ancillary charges may still be payable when you report.

7. Domicile and Home University

Maharashtra-domiciled candidates compete for State Level, Home University and Other Than Home University seats. Candidates from outside Maharashtra are generally considered for the All India quota seats, many of them on JEE (Main) scores.

  • Domicile is usually shown by a Domicile Certificate or the alternative proof the brochure lists.
  • "Home University" is the university region linked to you, usually through your Class XII institution. Confirm the definition in this year's brochure, because it decides which Home University seats you can take.

8. Worked examples

Example A: the incomplete OBC claim

Aman uploads his Caste Certificate but not his NCL certificate. His claim cannot be verified and he appears under OPEN on the provisional merit list, a much weaker position. He fixes it only in the grievance window, losing time.

Example B: the expired NCL

Sana's NCL was issued over a year ago for a scholarship and has passed its 31 March validity for this admission. She finds out during upload, with little time left to get a new one.

Example C: done right

Rhea reads the brochure the day results come out, sees her claim needs a Caste Certificate, a Caste Validity Certificate and a fresh NCL, and applies for the NCL renewal that week. Her documents are accepted first time.

10. Common mistakes

  1. Submitting the Caste Certificate without the Caste or Tribe Validity Certificate.
  2. Using an NCL that has passed its 31 March validity for this admission year.
  3. Confusing the EWS certificate with the TFWS income certificate.
  4. Applying for the validity certificate only after CAP registration opens.
  5. Assuming a PwD or Defence code is enough without the base category's documents.
  6. Leaving APAAR ID, UDID or domicile proof until the last week.
